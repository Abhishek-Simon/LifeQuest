## Brand & Style

This design system translates life-management, personal performance, and daily habits into a high-precision, tactical role-playing HUD. It serves ambitious individuals, engineers, and high-agency knowledge workers who want disciplined, game-inspired progression without childish gamification tropes. The aesthetic delivers an uncompromising balance of sleek modern product craftsmanship and focused game-state instrumentation.

The design movement synthesizes **Tactical Neomorphism & Dark Glassmorphism** with **Modern Product Precision**:
- **Dual Typographic Dialect:** Polished, contemporary product typography provides effortless legibility for daily operations, while razor-sharp, game-inspired display accents deliver impact on numeric readouts, levels, and battle conditions.
- **Engineered Substrates:** Dark modular panels, deep space backdrops, and laser-precise 1px micro-borders replace generic card elevations.
- **Measured Bioluminescence:** Strategic chromatic glows—violet, cyan, gold, and emerald—illuminate key active conduits, progression nodes, and dynamic status readouts without visual noise or terminal fatigue.

## Colors

The color system is calibrated for deep, immersive dark-mode execution. Matte obsidian surfaces anchor the interface, allowing semantic RPG hues to command immediate attention.

### Architectural Neutrals
- **Surface Base (`#0f131c`):** The primary canvas ground. Completely matte to eliminate visual glare.
- **Surface Deck (`#181c25`):** Structural sidebars, stationary navigation rails, and nested wells.
- **Surface Card (`#222734`):** Interactive modules, quest cards, active inventory items, and equipment slots.
- **Surface Overlay (`#2a3040`):** Modal dialogues, floating contextual tooltips, and elevated flyouts.
- **Borders (`#252b3b` / `#3b4356`):** Precision 1px division rules defining components and active boundaries.

### Semantic Functional Palette
- **Electric Violet (`#8b5cf6`):** Primary action token; governs player level, overall XP progression, rank upgrades, and intelligence attributes.
- **Cyber Cyan (`#06b6d4`):** Secondary tracker; governs mana pools, focus sessions, time-based countdowns, and agility metrics.
- **Warm Gold (`#f59e0b`):** Tertiary token; commands currency counters, loot crates, streak multipliers, and legendary achievements.
- **Emerald Vitality (`#10b981`):** Health bars, habit completion states, positive modifiers, and success cues.
- **Hazard Crimson (`#ef4444`):** Boss damage metrics, HP degradation, overdue penalties, and critical alerts.

## Typography

The typography implements a balanced dual-typeface hierarchy that removes monospace fatigue while preserving tactical edge:

1. **Primary Interface Font (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Handles headings, interface controls, quest logs, narrative descriptors, form labels, and navigation. Its geometric clarity and humanized proportions ensure high legibility and warmth across long work sessions.
2. **Display & Game Font (Rajdhani):** Applied specifically to quantified metrics, numeric HUD counters (Level, XP, Gold, Streak counts, boss HP), difficulty markers, stat parameters, and state tags. Its condensed, engineered glyphs evoke flight telemetry and high-tech instrument clusters.

### Implementation Rules
- **Numerical Regularity:** All elements using `Rajdhani` must set `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` to guarantee steady figures during tick-up animations and real-time countdowns.
- **Casing Standards:** Standard UI prose and headings use natural sentence or title case. Restrict uppercase treatment solely to `label-rpg` and `label-rpg-sm` (status tags, category slugs, and difficulty ratings). Avoid wide tracking on body copy.

## Layout & Spacing

Layout conforms to a fluid 12-column tactical arrangement designed for high information density without visual crowding.

### Screen Adaptations
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column system with `1rem` outer canvas padding. Persistent top HUD summary ribbon displays compact avatar level, HP, and mini-XP status. Tasks stack vertically.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column layout. Split interface with fixed player statistics rail and dual-column quest log.
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column grid framed by `2rem` screen margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Left rail (3 cols) houses Persistent Status and Attribute Matrix; Center deck (6 cols) displays Quest Engines and Encounter Timelines; Right rail (3 cols) handles Inventory, Buffs, and Streak logs.

Content density relies on consistent application of the `space-*` scale to maintain unified visual cadence across all instrument modules.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established through distinct surface tiers, hairline boundary rules, and selective luminous back-glows:

- **Tier 0 (Void Ground):** Matte Surface Base (`#0f131c`), void of shadow or radiance.
- **Tier 1 (Sub-Decks):** Surface Deck (`#181c25`) framed with a crisp `1px solid #252b3b`.
- **Tier 2 (Action Modules):** Surface Card (`#222734`) with `1px solid #252b3b`. When hovered or active, borders transition to `#3b4356` with a faint radiant trace: `box-shadow: 0 0 16px -4px rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.18)`.
- **Tier 3 (Floating HUD Overlays):** Modals, item tooltips, and boss alarms rely on Surface Overlay (`#2a3040`) backed by glass-like backdrop filters (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) and directional ambient glows: `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.8), 0 0 24px -6px rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.25)`.

## Shapes

The design uses `roundedness: 1` (`0.25rem` / `4px` baseline) to reflect precision engineering and tactical utility:

- **Baseline Geometry:** Cards, inputs, and standard buttons utilize subtle `4px` corner radii, maintaining clean alignment across data-dense matrices.
- **Tactical Cut Accents:** Specialized hero panels, quest claim anchors, and difficulty badges may use a 45-degree corner chamfer (3px–4px clip) on the top-right edge to communicate an authentic HUD framework.
- **Pill Badges:** Dynamic state chips and numerical micro-counters retain a slight `2px` micro-radius or full pill form strictly for rounded status dots.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Execute / Claim):** High-contrast violet (`#8b5cf6`) with dark surface text (`#0f131c`) set in `Plus Jakarta Sans` Bold (`headline-sm`). Border radius `4px`. Hover triggers `#a78bfa` with an ambient glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 16px rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.4)`).
- **Secondary (Inspect / Action):** Surface Card (`#222734`) with border `1px solid #252b3b` and text `#f1f5f9`. On hover, border shifts to `#06b6d4` with matching cyan text color.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** Transparent background with `#94a3b8` text, transitioning to `#f1f5f9` with an underlying hairline highlight on hover.

### Badges & RPG Chips
- Set in `label-rpg` (`Rajdhani` 600, uppercase).
- **XP / Level Tag:** Border `1px solid rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.35)`, background `rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.12)`, text `#c4b5fd`.
- **Focus / Mana Tag:** Border `1px solid rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.35)`, background `rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.12)`, text `#67e8f9`.
- **Gold / Streak Tag:** Border `1px solid rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.35)`, background `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.12)`, text `#fcd34d`.
- **Vitality Tag:** Border `1px solid rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.35)`, background `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.12)`, text `#6ee7b7`.

### Lists & Quest Cards
- Constructed on Surface Card (`#222734`) with an inset left boundary (3px solid) keyed to skill category (Violet, Cyan, Gold, or Emerald).
- Quest names use `headline-sm` in `Plus Jakarta Sans`. Associated rewards (`+250 XP`, `+50 GOLD`) sit flush-right in `stat-display-sm` using `Rajdhani` for high-impact readability.

### Form Inputs
- Surface Deck (`#181c25`) well with `1px solid #252b3b`. Text in `body-md` (`Plus Jakarta Sans`).
- Focus state activates an illuminated violet border (`#8b5cf6`) and a 1px inner ambient field. Placeholders render in `#64748b`.

### Checkboxes & Habit Triggers
- 20×20px squared boxes with `2px` micro-radii.
- Inactive: `#181c25` background, `1.5px solid #3b4356`.
- Completed: Crisp emerald fill (`#10b981`), charcoal checkmark (`#0f131c`), triggering a momentary emerald radial ripple and numerical stat increment.

### RPG Progress Meters
- Tracks feature recessed backgrounds (`#0f131c`) enclosed in a `1px solid #252b3b` boundary.
- Fill bars employ smooth saturated linear fills:
  - **Player Level / XP:** `#8b5cf6` with a bright `#a78bfa` leading edge.
  - **Focus Bar:** `#06b6d4` with trailing glow.
  - **Boss Encounter Health:** `#ef4444` to `#b91c1c` segmented with hairline dividers at 25% thresholds.
